What to look for before signing

  • Does the proposal include tear-off, disposal, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, cleanup, permits, and inspection?
  • How will soft decking, fascia, skylights, gutters, or solar panels be priced if discovered later?
  • Which product line, manufacturer warranty, and workmanship warranty are actually included?
  • What is the payment schedule, and what would legitimately change the final price?
  • Are you comparing the same roof system across each contractor proposal?

Why this matters

  • A cheaper bid is not always cheaper if the scope is thinner.
  • A vague proposal can create expensive surprises after tear-off.
  • Good contractors should be able to explain scope, materials, warranty, and payment terms clearly.
