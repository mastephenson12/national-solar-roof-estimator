Questions to take back to the contractor

A lower price may cover different work. This worksheet does not rank contractors or verify their claims.

  • Which blank items are included, excluded, or priced separately?
  • Are you quoting the same roof area and materials as the other proposals?
  • What could change the price, and how will extra work be approved?
  • Can you confirm the scope, warranty terms, and payment schedule in writing?
