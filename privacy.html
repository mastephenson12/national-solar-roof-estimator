Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Estimator use

You may calculate a roof-cost range without providing your name, email address, or phone number. Calculator selections remain in your browser unless you voluntarily request professional help.

Professional-help requests

If you enter an email address, check the consent box, and request professional help, MidSize AI sends the estimate details and email address to its automation provider so the request can be routed to roofing professionals. Those professionals may contact you about the project.

Service providers

MidSize AI may use hosting, automation, analytics, and customer-relationship services to operate the estimator. These providers process information only for the services they supply.

Your choices

Using the calculator does not require consent to marketing or contractor contact. Do not select the professional-help option unless you want your project information shared for follow-up.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit midsizeai.com.
