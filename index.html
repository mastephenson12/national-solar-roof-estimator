    Assumptions and limits

      Questions to ask before accepting a roofing proposal

      • Does the price include tear-off, disposal, permits, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and cleanup?
      • How is damaged decking priced if it is discovered after removal?
      • Which manufacturer, product line, warranty, and workmanship warranty are included?
      • Who handles solar panels, gutters, skylights, code upgrades, and final inspection?
      • What payment schedule applies, and what could legitimately change the final price?
